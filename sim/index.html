    Build notes

    On the desk
    —
    Knob to screen
    —

    The deck leans back 12.6° on four rubber pads. Each fader has its own FaderBuddy driver board soldered underneath; the boards plug into one another in a chain, and the cable leaves through the notch at the bottom of the back wall. Everything prints without supports.

    The CrowPanel goes in unmodified apart from its covers and side strips, turned so its USB-C / SD edge faces the faders (firmware Rotation 2). It presses into the display front, the back screws onto it, and two bolts through the back of the base hold the display down. Its wires run into the base beside the USB-C opening.

    Its USB-C port ends up inside the enclosure, so power it at the battery connector (B1) or at D2, at no more than 5.25 V, and take the FaderBuddy boards' 3.3 V logic supply from its GPIO header.
