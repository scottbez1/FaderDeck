# Changelog

FaderDeck's design version is in [`VERSION`](VERSION). It's in every download's file name, in each STL's header and
`LICENSE.txt`, and on the site. It goes up whenever a printed part changes: the minor number for changes that fit the same
hardware, the major number when the hardware list changes.

## 1.0

First public release.

- Screen-less decks: the corner feet's inner face is now 30° from horizontal (it was 45°), so it fits lower under the outer
  faders and the front feet sit 8 mm further forward. A knob at the front of its travel used to be 12 mm ahead of the front
  pads, so pressing it could tip the deck; it's now over their front edge. The face is a 58° overhang when the base prints
  top-down.
